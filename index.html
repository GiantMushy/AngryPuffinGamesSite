THE STUDIO

Four students, one game dev course, zero chill.

Angry Puffin Games is an Icelandic studio building party games for the phones already sitting on the table. We care about two things: getting everyone playing in under a minute, and giving them something worth shouting about.

MÁLÞÓF · 3–10 PLAYERS · ONE PHONE

An Icelandic party game inspired by parliamentary debate culture and the Morfís speech competition. Teams get a topic, give a short speech, sneak in corruption tasks for points — and at the end of the round, accuse the rivals they think were cheating.

01

State your case

The moderator picks a Versus or Scenario topic. Your team gets seconds to prepare and a short window to argue it like your life depends on it.

02

Get corrupt

Secret tasks worth big points: fake a sneezing fit, quote a philosopher who does not exist, compliment your opponent mid-insult. Do it without getting caught.

03

Point fingers

Round over. Accuse whoever you think was on the take. Correct accusations pay out — wild ones cost you, loudly, in front of everyone.

Clean like a banking app. Loud like a game.

Names in, topic up, speeches timed, scores settled. Nothing to learn, nothing to set up.
