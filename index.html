THE NEST

Four students, one game dev course, zero chill.

Angry Puffin Games is an Icelandic studio building party games for mobile. We care about two things: getting everyone playing together, and giving them something worth shouting about.

STATE YOUR CASE Local (one phone) · Online — coming soon

An Icelandic party game inspired by parliamentary debate culture and the Morfís speech competition hosted in Universities here in Iceland. Teams get a topic, give a minute speech, sneak in corruption tasks for points — and at the end of the round, accuse the rivals they think were completing their corruption.

State your case

The moderator picks a Versus or Scenario topic. Your team/you prepare and then argue it like your life depends on it.

Get corrupt

Secret tasks worth big points: fake a sneezing fit, quote a philosopher who does not exist, compliment your opponent mid-insult. Do it without getting caught.

Point fingers

Round over. Accuse whoever you think completed their tasks. Get the correct accusations and steal their points if not lose some of yours.

Clean and simple and easy to use.
